Parse time is ~33x higher with 2 code blocks/post than with 0, and grows
linearly with code-block count. Since parse_post_file() calls markdown_to_html()
inline (which invokes syntect highlighting), this isolates syntect syntax
highlighting as the dominant build cost -- ~97% of parse time, ~75-85% of total
build time on code-heavy corpora.

=== Conclusion ===

1. syntect highlighting is THE bottleneck. It runs single-threaded inside the
   serial parse loop. Biggest wins: (a) parallelize parsing across posts
   (rayon), and (b) reduce syntect per-line overhead / trim the SyntaxSet.
2. setup (~92ms fixed) is Steel engine init + SyntaxSet::load_defaults. Fixed
   cost, only worth attacking for tiny sites or watch-mode. An embedded/trimmed
   syntect dump would cut it.
3. render_index/rss/sitemap/assets are negligible (<1% combined). Not worth
   optimizing.
